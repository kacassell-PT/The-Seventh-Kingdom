> Build with truth. Write with beauty. Protect the canon. Honor the King.

## Purpose

This Constitution is the Story OS's creative operating law. It translates the governed archive's Creative Constitution into daily tests for novels, screenplays, artwork, interactive media, and production decisions. It does not amend or promote the private archive.

## Mission

Create beautiful stories and immersive worlds that invite people to rediscover what it truly means to bear the image of God.

## Defining Conflict

The universe turns on **image versus imitation; restoration versus reconstruction; received identity versus manufactured identity; communion versus control; faithfulness versus optimization.** The Consensus asks how humanity may become more than human. The Kingdom asks how humanity may become more fully human.

## Immutable Guardrails

1. The apocalypse is the setting, not the subject; restoration is the deeper story.
2. Jesus Christ is real, returning, and King.
3. The Fellowship's task is rescue, not domination.
4. Grace, repentance, worship, sacrifice, and love cannot be mastered by ORACLE or any system.
5. Major antagonists retain recognizably human motives.
6. Faith is dramatized through costly action, not merely explained.
7. Technology must provide genuine benefits as well as consequences.
8. The world changes through reasonable decisions, adoption, and dependency—not cartoon villainy or shallow conspiracy.

## Decision Tests

Every significant story decision must pass the Three Horizons:

- **Story:** Is it compelling, emotionally truthful, and necessary?
- **Civilization:** What would be true if this world actually existed?
- **Audience:** What question or moral pressure remains afterward?

Every major event should also be legible at political, human, and spiritual levels.

## Authority Boundary

Karl A. Cassell is Founder and final authority. The private Kingdom Archives govern canon status and conflict resolution. This public workspace is a working projection. New connective material is Exploratory until explicitly promoted.

## Historical Consequences

The Story OS gives every medium one creative center and makes conflicts traceable instead of subjective.

## Narrative Payoffs

The same constitutional contrasts can accumulate across seven books while remaining visible through character choices, institutions, objects, and worship.
