## Series Identity

**Title:** The Seventh Kingdom

**Mission:** To create beautiful stories and immersive worlds that invite people to rediscover what it truly means to bear the image of God.

**Format:** Seven-book speculative Christian epic with screen, cinematic video-book, companion, and interactive potential.

**Logline:** In a near-future world where artificial intelligence, programmable finance, predictive governance, biotechnology, surveillance, and religious convergence have fused into a benevolent-seeming global order, ordinary people discover that the deepest battle is not merely over how civilization is governed, but whether humanity will be reconstructed apart from the Creator or restored through communion with Him.

## Story Promise

The apocalypse is the setting, not the subject. The story follows human beings learning to see, become, witness, suffer, rescue, choose, and enter restoration under the true King.

## World

The Consensus grows through legitimate solutions, convenience, voluntary dependence, and institutional trust. The Harmony turns religious unity into a public good by treating exclusive truth claims as threats to peace. Against them, the Fellowship and followers of the Way preserve truth through worship, memory, hospitality, craftsmanship, discernment, and rescue. Houses of Memory are living archives and centers of formation.

## Core Systems

- **ORACLE:** powerful governing intelligence, spiritually blind and unable to master grace.
- **ARGUS:** surveillance and predictive-security architecture.
- **LifeCredit:** programmable financial permission system.
- **Continuity:** biotechnology and consciousness-transfer project.
- **Testament of Light:** ancient manuscript whose exact rules remain unresolved.

## Theme Engine

Every major storyline should test at least one of these questions:

- What makes a human being human?
- Who has the authority to name us?
- Can safety become a substitute for freedom?
- Can information preserve truth without living memory?
- What does rescue require when domination is easier?
- What can grace do that systems cannot predict?
- What kind of people are formed by worship, work, time, beauty, and communion?
