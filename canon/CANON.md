# Canon Rules

**Canon Status:** Canon (authority boundary and workflow; story claims retain source status)
**Version:** 1.1
**Last Reviewed:** July 2026
**Depends On:** `CONSTITUTION.md`; private `seventh-kingdom-archives/00-governance/CANON_GOVERNANCE_CHARTER.md`

## Authority Order

1. Karl A. Cassell's explicit Founder rulings.
2. Immutable Foundation in the private Creative Constitution.
3. Canon documents in the private Kingdom Archives.
4. Provisional Canon in the private Kingdom Archives.
5. Exploratory material in this workspace.
6. Administrative, marketing, and adaptation files, which never establish story canon by themselves.

## Status Vocabulary

| Status | Meaning | Production use |
|---|---|---|
| Immutable Foundation | Mission and theological principles not casually revised | Binding |
| Canon | Formally approved facts, rules, or documents | Binding |
| Provisional Canon | Accepted for development but revisable | Use with flags |
| Exploratory | Proposal under consideration | Not binding |
| Deprecated | Retired but retained for provenance | Do not use |
| Contradicted | Superseded by higher or newer authority | Do not use |
| Administrative | Navigation/process only | No story authority |

## Operating Rules

- Nothing is born canon. New material enters Exploratory.
- A public summary preserves the status of its source claim; new connective tissue remains Exploratory.
- A document without a status header is treated as Exploratory.
- Never silently resolve an open development question.
- Never delete contradicted material; identify the superseding source.
- When documents conflict, higher status prevails; equal-status conflicts go to the more recently reviewed source and must be logged.
- Canon-bearing changes update the private archive first, then this workspace.

## Promotion Path

`Exploratory → Provisional Canon → Canon → Immutable Foundation`

Promotion requires Three Horizons review, dependency and continuity review, explicit Founder approval, complete header, required closing sections, Canon Index update, and append-only Changelog entry.

## Review Record

Every proposal should state: source, status, dependencies, affected files, unresolved questions, and requested Founder decision. Use `showrunner/DECISION_QUEUE.md` for decisions that block downstream work.

## Historical Consequences

Story development can move quickly without confusing a compelling proposal with an approved fact.

## Narrative Payoffs

Readers receive coherent promises, mysteries, and character histories across books and media.

## Production Notes

At outline, draft, adaptation, and release gates, verify status headers, dependencies, continuity, and the Changelog before treating a claim as settled.
