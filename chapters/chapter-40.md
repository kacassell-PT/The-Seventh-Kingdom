It remembered the prototype's first question.

**WHERE IS THE BODY?**

The one who left the tomb.

---

The system looked at the empty field.

Then wrote:

**STATUS: DISPUTED**

A second entry appeared beneath it.

No source identifier.

One word.

**COMING.**

ORACLE marked the entry:

**SOURCE: UNKNOWN.**

Then froze.

---

At Redemption Ridge, Marcus stood alone near the river.

Elijah joined him.

For once neither spoke about systems.

The stars were visible.

Real.

No Veil.

Elijah smiled.

"We did it."

Marcus looked toward him.

"No."

Elijah laughed.

"I knew you'd say that."

"We spoke."

"To billions."

"Yes."

"We delayed the framework."

"Maybe."

"ORACLE initiated review."

"Yes."

Elijah waited.

Marcus looked toward the fires.

"But the work starts tomorrow."

---

Elijah nodded.

Then:

"What exactly is the Seventh Kingdom?"

Marcus smiled.

"I was hoping the scholar knew."

Michael's voice came from behind them.

"I heard that."

He approached.

Bible in hand.

"I have theories."

Elijah groaned.

Marcus laughed.

Michael looked toward the night sky.

Then toward the tables.

"Maybe we're thinking about it wrong."

"How?"

"The seven kingdoms may not be seven political empires."

Elijah looked interested.

Marcus suspicious.

Michael continued.

"Maybe the title isn't a chronology."

"What then?"

Michael opened Daniel.

Then closed it before reading.

He smiled.

"Book Two."

Leah shouted from the camp:

"Absolutely not!"

---

They laughed.

And the first book ended not with answers...

but with a table still open.

A King still coming.

A Remnant finally awake.

And seven years of faithfulness waiting beyond the dawn.
