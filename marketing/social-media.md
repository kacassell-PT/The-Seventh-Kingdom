## Standards

- Serve the reader before promoting the product.
- Never invent quotations, reactions, endorsements, or urgency.
- Label concept art and exploratory material accurately.
- Keep Perhaps Today ministry and Seventh Kingdom publishing identities appropriately distinct.
- Obtain permission and attribution for reader art or third-party material.
- Do not let a short-form post establish story canon.
