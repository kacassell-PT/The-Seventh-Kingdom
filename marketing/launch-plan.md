# Launch Plan

**Status:** Marketing development
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** July 2026

This plan begins with gates rather than assumed dates. Dates should be added only after manuscript, editorial, production, and distribution dependencies are known.

## Gate 1 — Foundation

- [ ] Final Book One title approved
- [ ] Manuscript approved
- [ ] Canon and continuity review complete
- [ ] Editorial process complete
- [ ] Rights and publishing path confirmed

## Gate 2 — Production

- [ ] Cover and interior complete
- [ ] Metadata and ISBN decisions complete
- [ ] Formats and distribution channels selected
- [ ] Proof copies reviewed
- [ ] Website and retailer pages ready

## Gate 3 — Audience preparation

- [ ] Positioning and back-cover copy approved
- [ ] Mailing-list path working
- [ ] Media kit complete
- [ ] Podcast and interview plan prepared
- [ ] Advance-reader strategy approved

## Gate 4 — Release

- [ ] Publication date confirmed
- [ ] Launch communications scheduled
- [ ] Purchase links tested
- [ ] Reader support and correction process ready

## Gate 5 — Stewardship

- [ ] Reviews and reader feedback collected ethically
- [ ] Corrections tracked
- [ ] Long-tail content continues serving readers
- [ ] Book Two communication does not outrun development
