# Publisher Query

**Status:** Business development draft
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** July 2026

Build query materials for literary agents, publishers, producers, or strategic partners here. Create a tailored version for each recipient rather than sending a generic package.

## Query framework

### Opening

- Recipient and reason for fit
- Book title and series position
- Genre and approximate word count
- Manuscript completion status

### Story pitch

- Protagonist
- Inciting disruption
- Central objective
- Escalating opposition
- Stakes and irreversible choice

### Series case

- Seven-book architecture
- Standalone satisfaction of Book One
- Differentiating theological and civilizational premise
- Adaptation potential without overstating commitments

### Creator biography

_To be written from Karl's approved public biography. Do not invent credentials, sales, audience figures, or personal experiences._

### Submission package

- [ ] Query tailored to recipient
- [ ] Synopsis
- [ ] Approved sample pages
- [ ] Word count verified
- [ ] Comparable titles researched
- [ ] Contact information checked
- [ ] Submission requirements followed exactly
