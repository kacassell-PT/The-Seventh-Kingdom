## Proposed site architecture

1. Home
2. The Series
3. Book One
4. The World
5. About Karl
6. Journal or updates
7. Media kit
8. Contact
9. Reader mailing list

## Home-page hierarchy

- Approved series promise
- Book One entry point
- Short world premise
- Primary call to action
- Visual contrast between the Consensus and the Kingdom
- Mailing-list invitation with a clear benefit

## Content boundaries

- Public worldbuilding must be drawn from approved canon.
- Protected mysteries remain absent or deliberately framed.
- Unpublished manuscript text requires Karl's approval.
- Perhaps Today ministry and The Seventh Kingdom Studios should remain clearly identified rather than casually blended.
- Privacy, accessibility, copyright, and mailing-list consent must be addressed before launch.
