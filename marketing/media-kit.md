# Media Kit

**Status:** Marketing development
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** July 2026

## Required assets

- Approved short, medium, and long project descriptions
- Approved short and long creator biographies
- Author headshots with photographer credit and usage rights
- Book-cover files at web and print resolution
- Series and studio logos
- Publication details and purchase links
- Interview topics
- Selected quotations from approved manuscript text
- Contact information
- Rights and attribution guidance

## Interview themes

Develop interview angles around faith, technology, memory, human formation, civilizational convergence, storytelling craft, and the difference between reconstruction and restoration.

## Verification

Every statistic, credential, quotation, release date, endorsement, and story claim must be checked before distribution.
