## Current verified frame

The Consensus integrates identity, finance, knowledge, security, health, and meaning through systems that genuinely improve ordinary life. Beneath that promise, public memory and moral agency become governable.

Elijah Cross carries responsibility for ORACLE. Michael Tesfaye enters a hidden archive and encounters the Testament of Light. Marcus Reed, haunted by Operation Jericho, learns protection without domination. Nathaniel Cole refuses to revise the record. Custodians such as Naomi Vale and Samuel Brooks confront evidence that prediction cannot account for conscience and grace.

Their stories move toward a first gathering whose exact recovered sequence remains incomplete.
