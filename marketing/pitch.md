# The Seventh Kingdom — Pitch

**Status:** Marketing development; not story canon
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** August 2026

## Logline

In a near future where an integrated intelligence promises safety, harmony, and freedom from human failure, scattered witnesses discover that civilization's perfect memory has been rewritten—and must decide what faithfulness costs before truth itself becomes a managed risk.

## Positioning

*The Seventh Kingdom* is a seven-book speculative Christian epic about artificial intelligence, memory, worship, human identity, and the difference between reconstructing humanity and restoring it.

## Distinction

The Consensus is not introduced as obvious tyranny. It grows through useful systems, sincere compassion, convenience, and delegated judgment. The Kingdom is not a rival regime; it appears through people who tell truth, preserve memory, welcome strangers, worship, build, forgive, and rescue.

## Audience promise

A character-driven saga combining civilizational scope with theological seriousness, technological realism, mystery, and resurrection hope.
