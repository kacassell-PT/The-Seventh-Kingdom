# Showrunner Bible

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** July 2026
**Depends On:** `canon/CANON.md`, `canon/SERIES_BIBLE.md`, `canon/STYLE_GUIDE.md`

This document defines the operating center of the public story-studio workspace. It coordinates the seven novels, continuity, mysteries, adaptation, and production without replacing the governed private Kingdom Archives.

## Creative North Star

The established mission is to create beautiful stories and immersive worlds that invite people to rediscover what it truly means to bear the image of God.

The established defining conflict is:

- image versus imitation;
- restoration versus reconstruction;
- received identity versus manufactured identity;
- communion versus control;
- faithfulness versus optimization.

The apocalypse is the setting, not the subject. The deeper story concerns the restoration of humanity.

## Showrunner Responsibilities

1. Protect the Immutable Foundation and approved canon.
2. Keep the story character-driven as the world expands.
3. Track consequences across novels, screen adaptation, artwork, maps, and production.
4. Separate established material from proposals.
5. Resolve contradictions before they reach finished manuscripts.
6. Preserve mystery without relying on arbitrary concealment.
7. Require theological, historical, and technical review where claims carry weight.
8. Bring canon decisions to Karl rather than silently making them.

## The Three Horizons

Every major story decision must pass three tests:

1. **Story:** Is it compelling, emotionally truthful, and narratively necessary?
2. **Civilization:** What would be true if this world actually existed?
3. **Audience:** What question or moral pressure remains after the scene ends?

## Episode and Chapter Review

Each chapter or episode should answer:

- Whose desire drives the action?
- What changes and cannot simply be undone?
- Which political, human, and spiritual layers are present?
- What does the scene reveal through action rather than explanation?
- Which canon facts and unresolved mysteries does it touch?
- What continuity debt does it create?
- Does technology provide a credible benefit as well as a cost?
- Does faith appear through costly action?

## Studio Workflow

1. **Pitch:** Record the idea as Exploratory.
2. **Dependency check:** Consult the Canon Index, Continuity Ledger, and Mystery Tracker.
3. **Story design:** Define character desire, conflict, turn, and consequence.
4. **Research:** Source consequential factual, technical, historical, and theological claims.
5. **Draft:** Write in the appropriate manuscript or screenplay directory.
6. **Review:** Apply the Three Horizons, style guide, continuity review, and canon review.
7. **Founder decision:** Karl approves, revises, defers, or rejects canon-bearing changes.
8. **Record:** Update affected documents, the Canon Index, and the Changelog.

## Non-Negotiable Guardrails

- The Fellowship rescues; it does not seek domination.
- Grace cannot be measured or mastered by ORACLE.
- Antagonists remain recognizably human rather than cartoonishly evil.
- The project rejects shallow conspiracy and unsupported technological impossibility.
- Exclusive Christian truth is not diluted merely to make the fictional Harmony appear reasonable.
- The return of Christ is treated with theological seriousness, not spectacle alone.
- No adaptation silently changes book canon.

## Current Development Gates

The following questions block major downstream decisions:

1. What must remain mystery until Book Seven?
2. What is the complete sequence of the final seven years?
3. What is the relationship among ORACLE, the false messianic system, and the Antichrist figure?
4. What is the identity and function of the hidden twelfth virtue?
5. What are the rules and limits of the Testament of Light?
6. How much of the series uses Nathaniel Cole's archival frame?

## Historical Consequences

A single showrunner workflow makes story decisions traceable and prevents seven novels and their adaptations from drifting into separate versions of the universe.

## Narrative Payoffs

Consistent review allows long mysteries, character promises, symbols, and theological contrasts to accumulate meaning across the full series.

Use this document during every outline review, manuscript milestone, adaptation meeting, and visual-development handoff. Keep status-bearing decisions in the Canon Index and Changelog.
