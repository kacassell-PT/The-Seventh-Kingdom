## Scene Continuity Entry

For every completed chapter or screenplay sequence, record:

- date and location;
- viewpoint character;
- characters present;
- what each character knows before and after;
- injuries, objects, documents, promises, and unresolved actions;
- technologies used and their demonstrated limits;
- mystery clues planted or paid off;
- canon documents touched;
- downstream chapters requiring updates.

## Contradiction Protocol

1. Stop propagation of the disputed fact.
2. Identify the highest-status and most recently reviewed sources.
3. Record every dependent scene or document.
4. Bring canon-impacting resolutions to Karl.
5. Update the Changelog and affected references after decision.

## Historical Consequences

A continuity ledger prevents silent revision and preserves cause and effect as the project expands across seven books and multiple media.

## Narrative Payoffs

Characters retain credible memories, wounds, loyalties, and knowledge states, allowing long promises and consequences to land honestly.

## Production Notes

During screen adaptation, create a separate adaptation column rather than changing novel continuity to match production constraints.
