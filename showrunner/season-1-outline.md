## Season objective

Make viewers desire the Consensus before revealing its spiritual and human cost; converge the principal storylines; open the Archives, Testament, Jericho, ORACLE, and Twelve mysteries; end with irreversible belonging.

## Proposed movements

1. **Ordinary convergence:** benefits, Carters, identity, LifeCredit.
2. **Hidden records:** Michael's archive, Nathaniel's refusal, Sarah and Leah's evidence.
3. **Shepherd and Custodians:** Marcus, Naomi, Samuel, and the moral divide inside enforcement.
4. **Gathering:** characters move west and the first identities are named.
