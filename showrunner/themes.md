# Themes

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** August 2026
**Depends On:** `../canon/CONSTITUTION.md`; `../canon/SERIES_BIBLE.md`

## Governing themes

- Image versus imitation
- Restoration versus reconstruction
- Communion versus control
- Memory versus curated information
- Formation versus optimization
- Resurrection versus continuity
- Rescue versus domination
- Received identity versus manufactured identity
- Faithfulness versus efficiency
- Cultivation versus consumption

## Theme test

A theme must become a choice, relationship, practice, cost, or consequence. It should never survive only as dialogue.

## Historical Consequences

Themes govern civilization design as well as individual arcs.

## Narrative Payoffs

The same conflict can recur at personal, institutional, and spiritual scales.

## Production Notes

Track the dominant and secondary theme for each chapter and episode.
