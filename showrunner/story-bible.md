## Story promise

A civilizational epic about whether humanity will be reconstructed apart from its Creator or restored through communion with Him.

## Central question

> Whose image do you bear?

## Dramatic engine

The Consensus offers real benefits and increasing dependency. The Kingdom becomes visible through truth, worship, memory, hospitality, craftsmanship, costly love, and rescue. Characters converge as institutional records, predictions, and spiritual reality cease to agree.

## Core ensemble

Elijah Cross, Marcus Reed, Michael Tesfaye, Nathaniel Cole, Naomi Vale, Sarah Navarro, Leah Morgan, Samuel Brooks, the Carter family, Solomon Vey, Miriam Kaine, and ORACLE. The Gardener remains protected mystery.

## Series destination

Christ returns as King; the story moves beyond collapse into restoration. The exact final-seven-years sequence and Book Seven reveal reserve remain unresolved.

## Historical Consequences

One compact story bible aligns character, world, theology, and series destination.

## Narrative Payoffs

Long mysteries and relationships can accumulate meaning across all seven books.
