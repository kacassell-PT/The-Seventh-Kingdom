This queue concentrates the few decisions that unlock the most downstream work. A listed option is not canon until Karl decides and the decision is recorded.

## Critical Priority

### 1. Book Seven mystery reserve

**Decision needed:** What truths must remain unrevealed until Book Seven?

**Why first:** This controls foreshadowing, viewpoint limits, Testament revelations, Cole's archive, ORACLE's arc, and the endings of Books One–Six.

**Required output:** A short protected list with what readers may know at the end of each preceding book.

## Important Priorities

### 2. Final-seven-years sequence

Decide when the seven-year clock begins and whether all seven books occur within it. This controls chronology, ages, institutional change, and pacing.

### 3. ORACLE and the final false order

Define what ORACLE is and is not in relation to Harmony, the false messianic system, and the Antichrist figure. Preserve ORACLE's established spiritual limitation.

### 4. Testament of Light rules

Define what the Testament can reveal, to whom, under what conditions, and what it can never do. Prevent puzzle-box or magical-object drift.

## Deferred but Tracked

- Identity and narrative function of the hidden twelfth virtue.
- Extent of Nathaniel Cole's archival framing.
- Final fates of ORACLE, Solomon Vey, Miriam Kaine, and Naomi Vale.
- Geographic and cultural anchors of the principal Houses of Memory.
- Portrayal limits for supernatural beings and spiritual gifts.
- Relationship between the working labels *Calling* and *The Fifth Remnant* for Book One.
