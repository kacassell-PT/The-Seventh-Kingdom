# Showrunner Structure

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.2
**Last Reviewed:** July 2026
**Depends On:** `canon/CONSTITUTION.md`; `canon/CANON.md`; `canon/SERIES_BIBLE.md`; existing files in `showrunner/`

## Purpose

The showrunner layer is the operating center of Story OS. It coordinates story, continuity, mysteries, characters, timeline, adaptation, research, and Founder decisions without becoming a competing canon archive.

## Control Tower

| Function | Authoritative file | Operating question |
|---|---|---|
| Creative law | `canon/CONSTITUTION.md` | Does this belong to the story? |
| Canon authority | `canon/CANON.md` | What status does it have? |
| Series promise | `canon/SERIES_BIBLE.md` | What is the saga about? |
| Book/season plan | `showrunner/SERIES_PLAN.md` | What must this installment accomplish? |

| Adaptation | `screenplay/` | What changes by medium, and is it labeled? |

## Decision Flow

1. **Intake:** classify the idea and mark it Exploratory.
2. **Dependency check:** inspect canon, continuity, timeline, mystery, character, and research references.
3. **Design:** define desire, conflict, turn, consequence, and three-horizon value.
4. **Draft:** write in the narrowest appropriate workspace.
5. **Review:** test canon, theology, realism, style, continuity, and mystery protection.
6. **Founder gate:** surface only decisions requiring Karl's authority.
7. **Propagate:** update dependent ledgers and references.
8. **Record:** update Canon Index and Changelog when status or canon-bearing structure changes.

## Review Gates

- **Concept gate:** premise serves the Constitution.
- **Outline gate:** book/episode has a human spine and downstream consequences.
- **Draft gate:** scene is concrete, character-anchored, and not exposition-only.
- **Continuity gate:** dates, knowledge, objects, injuries, technology, and promises reconcile.
- **Canon gate:** status and authority are explicit.
- **Release gate:** public claims match the governed archive and unresolved material is not marketed as settled.

## Working Rhythm

Use one active source of truth per domain. Prefer small, linked records over duplicated summaries. When a change crosses domains, update the originating record first, then the Continuity Ledger, then downstream documents.

## Historical Consequences

The studio can scale across novels, television, cinematic video-book, games, and companion works without losing authority boundaries.

## Narrative Payoffs

Long-range mysteries and character promises are protected while each installment remains usable to its immediate audience.

## Production Notes

The Showrunner Bible, Series Plan, Continuity Ledger, Mystery Tracker, and Decision Queue are the minimum weekly operating set. Review the Story Constitution and Canon Rules before any of them.
