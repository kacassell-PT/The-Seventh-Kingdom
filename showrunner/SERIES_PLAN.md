**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** July 2026
**Depends On:** `canon/SERIES_BIBLE.md`, `showrunner/SHOWRUNNER_BIBLE.md`

This is the public workspace's planning view of the seven-book architecture. Titles and core arcs below mirror Provisional Canon in the governed archive. Detailed movements, timelines, and endings remain subject to Founder review.

## Series Movement

The series moves from **seeing**, to **becoming**, to **witnessing**, to **suffering**, to **rescuing**, to **choosing**, to **restoration**.

| Book | Working title | Governing arc | Development state |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Calling / *The Fifth Remnant* | The world is revealed; key characters awaken and converge | Active manuscript development; final title relationship requires Founder confirmation |
| 2 | Formation | Resistance without spiritual formation reproduces what it opposes | Architecture only |
| 3 | Witness | Truth becomes public through costly testimony | Architecture only |
| 4 | Persecution | Faithful witness is reframed as extremism and public danger | Architecture only |
| 5 | Harvest | Awakening, deception, rescue, and irreversible choices | Architecture only |
| 6 | The Great Separation | Allegiances become unmistakable | Architecture only |
| 7 | The Seventh Kingdom | Christ returns; restoration begins in the first forty days | Architecture only |

## Series-Wide Throughlines

### Human

The story remains rooted in people, families, communities, grief, courage, compromise, repentance, and costly faithfulness.

### Civilizational

The Consensus grows through reasonable solutions, voluntary dependence, convenience, institutional trust, and the integration of identity, economy, knowledge, security, and meaning.

### Spiritual

The Kingdom forms people through worship, memory, hospitality, craftsmanship, community, truth, and rescue. Its victory is not domination but faithful witness under the true King.

## Book-One Development Target

Book One must accomplish five tasks without becoming an encyclopedia:

1. Make the Consensus attractive enough to be credible.
2. Reveal the first fracture through human consequences.
3. Introduce the central cast through converging needs, not roster presentation.
4. Establish that the two Archives do not match.
5. End with a choice that makes returning to ordinary life impossible.

## Cross-Book Control Points

Before any later-book outline is treated as stable, decide:

- the final-seven-years sequence;
- the Book Seven mystery reserve;
- the Testament's rules;
- ORACLE's relationship to the final false order;
- the fate boundaries for ORACLE, Solomon Vey, Miriam Kaine, and Naomi Vale;
- the identity and dramatic purpose of the hidden twelfth virtue.

## Historical Consequences

The seven-book structure gives the studio a shared planning horizon and prevents a compelling local idea from breaking the saga's final destination.

## Narrative Payoffs

Each book can deliver a complete emotional movement while advancing the civilizational convergence and the restoration of humanity.

## Production Notes

Treat each book as both a novel and a possible screen season, but protect the novel's internal form. Adaptation planning belongs in `screenplay/` and must reference rather than overwrite this plan.
