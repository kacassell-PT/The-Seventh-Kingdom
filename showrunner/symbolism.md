# Symbolism

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** August 2026
**Depends On:** `../legacy/THE_RULE_OF_SYMBOLS.md`; private Creative Constitution Article IX

| Symbol | Governing meaning |
|---|---|
| Bread | Provision, hospitality, communion |
| Oil | Consecration, healing, presence |
| Water | Life, cleansing, baptism, memory |
| Fire | Refinement, judgment, danger, hope |
| Trees | Roots, fruit, patient growth |
| Stone | Covenant, permanence, witness |
| Light | Presence rather than information |
| White lily | Purity, resurrection, grace |
| Shepherd's staff | Protection without domination |
| Long Table | Communion and belonging |

## Historical Consequences

A protected symbolic language preserves cultural continuity across media.

## Narrative Payoffs

Symbols gain emotional force through repetition and changed context.

## Production Notes

Deepen approved meanings; do not repurpose them casually.
