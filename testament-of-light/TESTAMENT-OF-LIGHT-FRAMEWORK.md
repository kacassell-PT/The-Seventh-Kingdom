# Testament of Light Framework

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** July 2026
**Depends On:** `canon/CONSTITUTION.md`; `canon/SERIES_BIBLE.md`; `canon/THEOLOGY_REFERENCE.md`; private archive Open Development Question #4

## Purpose

This framework organizes development of the Testament of Light without turning it into a puzzle box, magical tool, second Scripture, or computational oracle. Its exact origin, authority, mechanism, limits, and revelation sequence remain open Founder decisions.

## Established Boundary

The Testament is an ancient manuscript whose text reveals itself according to obedience, readiness, and spiritual perception. It is a living witness that truth cannot be reduced to possession, extraction, or computation. It must never compete with, amend, or supersede Scripture.

## Five-Part Design Model

For every proposed Testament appearance, record:

1. **Reader:** Who encounters it, and what do they believe they are seeking?
2. **Condition:** What obedience, readiness, suffering, humility, or love precedes perception?
3. **Revelation:** What is seen, heard, remembered, or understood?
4. **Misreading:** What does the character want the text to authorize?
5. **Fruit:** What costly action, repentance, service, or refusal follows?

## Operating Constraints

- It cannot be forced by power, ownership, code, violence, or extraction.
- It does not provide exhaustive prediction or eliminate moral choice.
- It does not reward curiosity detached from faithfulness.
- It does not function as a weapon, shortcut, or answer key.
- Its revelations must remain subordinate to Scripture and subject to theological review.
- Ambiguity may remain when clarity would turn obedience into technique.
- A perceived revelation must be tested by truth, fruit, community, and costly faithfulness—not private certainty alone.

## Mystery Protection

Never reveal origin, complete rules, or final meaning merely to resolve reader confusion. Consult `showrunner/MYSTERY_TRACKER.md` and the Book Seven mystery reserve. Every revelation should deepen the central conflict between received truth and manufactured control.

## Historical Consequences

The Testament remains a witness within the world rather than a mechanism that collapses mystery into information.

## Narrative Payoffs

Its progressive appearances can expose character, test communities, and distinguish obedience from possession across the series.

## Production Notes

Keep visual and sonic treatment restrained. Record every proposed fragment verbatim in a controlled ledger, track translation and theological review, and never imply that a prop or interface can manufacture spiritual perception.
