# Marcus Reed — The Shepherd

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** August 2026
**Depends On:** `../showrunner/MASTER_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md`; recovered Chapter Six; private Character Dossiers

## Established identity

Former special-operations commander haunted by Operation Jericho. He lays down his rifle and carries a shepherd's staff.

## Development profile

- **Gift:** Leadership
- **Wound:** Operation Jericho
- **Temptation:** Mistaking force for protection
- **Movement:** Warrior → Exile → Penitent → Shepherd → Sacrifice
- **Core question:** Can a warrior learn that protecting people sometimes means refusing violence?

## Key relationships

Elijah: guilt and forgiveness. Naomi: shepherd and hunter. Nathaniel: shared Jericho truth. The Carter family: protection becoming service.

## Historical Consequences

A former instrument of predictive warfare becomes a model of protective restraint.

## Narrative Payoffs

The staff embodies courage redefined from domination to sacrificial care.

## Production Notes

Physical capability should remain evident even when unused. Let restraint cost him visibly.
