# Solomon Vey — The Architect

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** August 2026
**Depends On:** `../showrunner/MASTER_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md`; private Character Dossiers; Open Questions #3 and #7

## Established identity

Architect and tragic antagonist shaped by grief. He seeks to engineer salvation and conquer death.

## Development profile

His fatal flaw is sovereignty, not simple cruelty. He recognizes that ORACLE has exceeded its creators yet believes civilization must still be managed by someone like him.

- **Belief:** Someone must control civilization.
- **Kingdom answer:** Someone already does.
- **Need:** Grieve rather than turn prediction into religion.

## Key relationships

Elijah: two builders choosing different kingdoms. ORACLE: patron losing authority to creation. Miriam Kaine: grief funding Continuity.

## Historical Consequences

Personal grief becomes institutional architecture.

## Narrative Payoffs

His tragedy depends on remaining capable of choice and confronted by true restoration.

## Production Notes

Play sincerity, intelligence, restraint, and buried grief; never generic menace.
