# Elijah Cross — The Builder

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** August 2026
**Depends On:** `../showrunner/MASTER_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md`; private Character Dossiers

## Established identity

Former architect of ORACLE and behavioral governance; a repentant fugitive. His governing question is whether a man who helped build Babylon can become useful to the Kingdom.

## Development profile

- **Gift:** Creation
- **Wound:** Guilt
- **Temptation:** Believing intelligence can repair what intelligence corrupted
- **Movement:** Architect → Fugitive → Penitent → Disciple → Builder of refuge
- **Redemption test:** Build something that does not control people rather than merely destroy ORACLE.

## Key relationships

Marcus: guilt and forgiveness. Solomon: two builders serving different kingdoms. ORACLE: creator and creation, responsibility and repentance. Leah: rescue architecture.

## Historical Consequences

Elijah's designs helped make integrated governance possible; his later work may create infrastructure for rescue.

## Narrative Payoffs

His redemption turns from undoing the past toward faithful stewardship.

## Production Notes

Performance should carry intelligence restrained by shame; craftsmanship replaces mastery over time.
