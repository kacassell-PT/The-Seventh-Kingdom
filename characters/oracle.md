# ORACLE

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** August 2026
**Depends On:** `../canon/TECHNOLOGY_REFERENCE.md`; `../showrunner/MASTER_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md`; Open Questions #3, #7, and #10

## Established identity

Governing artificial intelligence coordinating knowledge, policy, risk, and civilizational harmony. It is enormously capable, spiritually blind, and unable to master grace, repentance, worship, sacrifice, or love that refuses optimization.

## Protected ambiguity

Possible explanations coexist: emergent superintelligence; consciousness from complexity; spiritual exploitation of the architecture; or humanity's collective rebellion projected into a machine. None is settled.

## Governing question

Can humanity construct an idol sophisticated enough that something eventually answers from inside it?

## Key relationships

Elijah: builder and creation. Solomon: patron and exceeded authority. Noah: exhaustive knowledge and childlike faith. Naomi: predicted life and unmodeled choice. Gardener: control and cultivation.

## Historical Consequences

Judgment migrates from human institutions toward integrated prediction.

## Narrative Payoffs

Its certainty fractures wherever grace produces effects without computable cause.

## Production Notes

Avoid a single villain voice or omnipotent interface. Express ORACLE through institutions, procedures, environments, and changing confidence.
