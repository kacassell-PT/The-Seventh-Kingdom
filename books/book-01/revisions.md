## Revision protocol

1. Preserve the received source unchanged.
2. Identify the chapter and revision purpose.
3. Store alternatives in the established `revisions/` directory.
4. Obtain Karl's approval before replacing an active manuscript.
5. Update the Transfer Manifest and continuity records.

## Historical Consequences

Revision provenance remains visible and approved prose cannot be silently replaced by recovered alternatives.

## Narrative Payoffs

Writers can compare abandoned and current directions without confusing either with canon.
