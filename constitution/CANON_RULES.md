## Source-Proposed Rules

### Rule 1 — Approved manuscript authority

Only author-approved manuscript chapters may become locked story continuity. Under the governed archive’s vocabulary, “Locked Canon” is not a separate status and must be translated into an explicit Founder-approved **Canon** ruling before use.

### Rule 2 — Planning remains provisional

Ideas discussed in planning remain working material until approved and properly registered. Under the governed vocabulary, new planning enters as **Exploratory**, not Canon. Appearance in a manuscript may support promotion but does not automatically create it.

### Rule 3 — Retcons are a last resort

Whenever possible, new material should deepen previous events rather than overwrite them. Any contradiction or demotion follows the private Charter’s Continuity Impact Review and append-only record.

### Rule 4 — Prophecy must have a controlled arc

Every introduced prophecy should have:

1. introduction;
2. development;
3. fulfillment or an explicitly governed reason its fulfillment remains beyond the narrative.

The Story OS should know the intended resolution before publication even when readers will not learn it for several books. Biblical prophecy must not be reduced to a plot mechanism or fixed fictional chronology without theological and Founder review.

### Rule 5 — Mysteries require governed answers

Every major mystery should have an answer known to the Story OS, even when withheld from readers. If the final answer is intentionally unresolved, that choice must be explicit, protected, and approved rather than accidental.

## Binding Status Translation

| ChatGPT term | Governed treatment |
|---|---|
| Locked Canon | Canon only after explicit Founder approval and registration |
| Working Canon | Exploratory by default; Provisional Canon only after Founder promotion |
| Story OS answer | Controlled development answer; not public canon until approved |

Nothing is born canon. The private `seventh-kingdom-archives` Canon Governance Charter controls promotion, demotion, conflict resolution, dependencies, and the Changelog.

## Historical Consequences

The useful planning principles from the ChatGPT conversation are preserved without creating conflicting canon vocabulary.

## Narrative Payoffs

Prophecies, mysteries, manuscript facts, and later revelations can develop coherently without silent retcons.

## Production Notes

Track prophecies in the Mystery Tracker, manuscript facts in the Continuity Ledger, status in the Canon Index, and every promotion or contradiction in the append-only Changelog.
