## Mission Statement

The Seventh Kingdom exists to tell compelling stories that awaken readers to the eternal Kingdom of God by exploring the collision between technological power, human freedom, truthful witness, and faithful discipleship.

Through unforgettable characters and cinematic storytelling, the series invites readers to ask not merely what the future will become, but to whom their allegiance ultimately belongs.

## Operating Meaning

The mission calls the studio to:

- tell excellent stories rather than disguise arguments as fiction;
- make spiritual questions visible through believable people and costly choices;
- examine technology as morally consequential without treating it as inherently evil;
- preserve truthful witness under pressure;
- direct the saga toward Christ without assuming every reader begins from Christian belief.

## Historical Consequences

A clear mission gives every novel, adaptation, companion work, and studio decision a common test of purpose.

## Narrative Payoffs

The series can explore technological power and civilizational change while keeping allegiance, witness, discipleship, and the image of God at its center.
