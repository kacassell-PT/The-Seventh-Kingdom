## Article I — Purpose

The Seventh Kingdom exists to tell unforgettable stories that awaken readers to the eternal Kingdom of God by exploring the collision between technological power, human freedom, truthful witness, and faithful discipleship.

The series exists to entertain.

The series exists to challenge.

The series exists to provoke.

The series exists to inspire.

Most importantly, the series exists to glorify Christ through excellent storytelling.

## Article II — Mission

To create a fictional universe that helps readers wrestle with timeless spiritual questions through believable characters living in extraordinary circumstances.

The goal is never propaganda. The goal is transformation through story.

## Article III — Vision

To develop a Christ-centered fictional universe capable of expanding into novels, companion books, devotionals, educational resources, podcasts, visual adaptations, and other creative works while remaining faithful to one coherent theological and narrative foundation.

## Article IV — Audience

The series is written for:

- Christians seeking thoughtful fiction;
- readers of speculative fiction who enjoy philosophical and moral questions;
- readers interested in artificial intelligence, surveillance, ethics, and technology;
- people who may never read theology but will engage a compelling story.

The books should never assume the reader already agrees with the worldview. The narrative should invite exploration rather than demand conclusions.

## Article V — The Creative Covenant

Every contributor to The Seventh Kingdom should strive to:

- pursue excellence rather than shortcuts;
- protect continuity;
- honor the internal logic of the world;
- respect the intelligence of the reader;
- allow mystery to exist without rushing to explain it;
- keep character growth at the center of the narrative;
- remember that spectacle should never replace substance.
