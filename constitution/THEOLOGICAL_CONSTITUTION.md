## Foundational Convictions

1. **God remains sovereign over history.** Human systems may become powerful, but they never replace God’s authority.
2. **Every person bears the image of God.** Technology may imitate humanity but cannot create the divine image.
3. **Truth exists independent of power.** Institutions can suppress truth, but they cannot redefine reality.
4. **Redemption is personal and relational.** It cannot be engineered, automated, or mass-produced.
5. **Freedom is essential to love.** A perfectly controlled world cannot produce genuine love or worship.
6. **Hope is rooted in God’s promises rather than human predictions.**
7. **The Kingdom of God grows through faithful witness, not coercion.**

## Recurring Theological Themes

Throughout the seven books, the story may repeatedly explore:

- repentance;
- grace;
- sacrifice;
- reconciliation;
- truth;
- faithfulness;
- resurrection;
- identity;
- shepherding;
- discipleship;
- the cost of witness;
- hope under persecution.

These themes should emerge naturally through characters and story rather than extended exposition.
