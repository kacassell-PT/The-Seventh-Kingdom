# Vision

**Canon Status:** Exploratory (ChatGPT source proposal; Founder adoption required)
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** July 2026
**Depends On:** `MISSION.md`; `THE_SEVENTH_KINGDOM_CONSTITUTION.md`; private Kingdom Archives Creative Constitution

## Vision Statement

To build a Christ-centered fictional universe whose stories, companion works, adaptations, and community inspire readers to think deeply, live faithfully, and recognize the difference between the kingdoms of this world and the Kingdom that cannot be shaken.

## Long-Range Expression

The Seventh Kingdom may expand through:

- seven core novels;
- companion books and devotionals;
- educational resources;
- podcasts and audio experiences;
- visual and screen adaptations;
- interactive and immersive works;
- a community formed around thoughtful engagement with truth, technology, freedom, witness, and hope.

Expansion must serve coherence rather than scale for its own sake. Every medium remains accountable to the same theological and narrative foundation, while respecting the strengths and limits of its form.

## Authority Note

This vision is a proposal recovered from the live ChatGPT development conversation. It does not supersede the private Kingdom Archives or promote any franchise format, theological claim, or story detail by itself.

## Historical Consequences

The project is framed as a durable fictional universe rather than a single manuscript or isolated product.

## Narrative Payoffs

Readers can encounter the same coherent conflict and hope across distinct stories and media without receiving competing versions of the world.

## Production Notes

Evaluate every proposed medium by mission fit, canon risk, audience value, stewardship cost, and whether the story is genuinely strengthened by the form.
