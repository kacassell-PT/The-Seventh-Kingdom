# Doctrine of Compassion

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** August 2026
**Depends On:** `doctrine-human-flourishing.md`; `../../canon/CONSTITUTION.md`

The Consensus understands compassion as the prevention of distress, exclusion, risk, and offense through coordinated intervention. It seeks humane outcomes and often achieves them.

Its danger appears when compassion loses truth, consent, moral agency, and limits. Protection becomes management; disagreement becomes harm; costly love is replaced by frictionless care.

## Historical Consequences

Coercive policy can be administered in therapeutic language by sincere people.

## Narrative Payoffs

Custodians may harm characters while honestly believing they are rescuing them.

## Production Notes

Performance should emphasize concern, patience, and procedural confidence rather than cruelty.
