# Consensus Creed

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** August 2026
**Depends On:** `doctrine-human-flourishing.md`; `doctrine-identity.md`; `doctrine-compassion.md`; `doctrine-correction.md`; `doctrine-information.md`; `doctrine-hope.md`

> We belong to one humanity.
>
> What harms one endangers all.
>
> What can be known should be shared.
>
> What can be prevented should not be suffered.
>
> What divides us must be healed.
>
> What limits humanity may be overcome.
>
> Together, we become whole.

This proposed creed condenses the Consensus's strongest moral case. It is not approved dialogue or public doctrine until Karl adopts it.

## Historical Consequences

A common moral vocabulary unifies institutions without requiring overt political conquest.

## Narrative Payoffs

Each line can become both sincere promise and tragic inversion.

## Production Notes

If used, present it through education, ceremony, interfaces, and care settings—not rallies alone.
