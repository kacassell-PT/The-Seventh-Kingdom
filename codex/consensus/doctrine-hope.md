# Doctrine of Hope

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** August 2026
**Depends On:** `doctrine-human-flourishing.md`; `../../canon/THEOLOGY_REFERENCE.md`

Consensus hope is the confidence that sufficient intelligence, coordination, and technology can overcome disorder, scarcity, conflict, disease, and eventually death.

Kingdom hope is received rather than engineered. It does not reject healing or knowledge; it refuses to make them sovereign. Resurrection is not continuity, and salvation is not optimization.

## Historical Consequences

Civilizational trust migrates from covenant and providence toward prediction and technical administration.

## Narrative Payoffs

Solomon Vey and Miriam Kaine embody hope transformed into sovereignty.

## Production Notes

Let Consensus hope be beautiful enough that rejection costs something.
