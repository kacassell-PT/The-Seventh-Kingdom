# Doctrine of Correction

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** August 2026
**Depends On:** `doctrine-compassion.md`; `../../showrunner/MYSTERY_TRACKER.md`

Correction within the Consensus begins with education, context, incentives, and care plans. Punishment is publicly disfavored; maladaptive belief and behavior are redirected toward safe participation.

The governing danger is preemption: the system acts on predicted trajectories before a person has chosen. Repentance is confused with compliance, and conscience with risk.

## Historical Consequences

Justice shifts from adjudicating acts toward managing predicted persons.

## Narrative Payoffs

Samuel Brooks and Naomi Vale confront the difference between statistical correctness and human justice.

## Production Notes

Show layered escalation: notification, coaching, limitation, review, then retrieval.
