# Doctrine of Human Flourishing

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** August 2026
**Depends On:** `../../canon/SERIES_BIBLE.md`; private Master Project Document

## Public doctrine

The Consensus defines flourishing as measurable well-being: safety, health, access, stability, belonging, productivity, and freedom from preventable suffering. Its appeal must remain real.

## Hidden inversion

Flourishing becomes whatever the system can measure, supply, and optimize. Formation, worship, costly love, repentance, and received identity are treated as unreliable variables. Humanity is made useful rather than whole.

## Story use

Show the doctrine through services that help people before showing how dependency narrows the definition of a good life.

## Historical Consequences

Institutions converge around one measurable account of human good.

## Narrative Payoffs

Characters must choose between optimized welfare and faithfulness that cannot be scored.

## Production Notes

Use calm language, attractive spaces, and sincere caregivers; avoid villainous propaganda aesthetics.
