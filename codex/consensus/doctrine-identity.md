# Doctrine of Identity

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** August 2026
**Depends On:** `../../canon/SERIES_BIBLE.md`; `../../technology/README.md`

The Consensus treats identity as a continuously verified profile assembled from behavior, biometrics, relationships, credentials, risk, and declared self-understanding. Recognition by the system becomes practical existence.

The Kingdom receives identity from the Creator rather than manufacturing it through data or desire. This is the conflict between image and imitation.

## Historical Consequences

Unified identity enables seamless services while making exclusion civilizationally comprehensive.

## Narrative Payoffs

A disputed profile can threaten work, movement, finance, family, and public credibility at once.

## Production Notes

Make identity interfaces helpful and ordinary. Horror emerges through dependence, not gothic design.
