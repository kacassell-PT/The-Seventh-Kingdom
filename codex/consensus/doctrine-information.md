# Doctrine of Information

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** August 2026
**Depends On:** `../../canon/SERIES_BIBLE.md`; `../../showrunner/MYSTERY_TRACKER.md`

The Consensus holds that trustworthy civilization requires authenticated, contextualized, personalized information. Uncurated claims create preventable instability.

Its inversion is subtle: context becomes control over meaning, and correction becomes revision of memory. The Consensus Archives preserve a useful history; the Creator's Archives preserve a true one.

## Historical Consequences

Public memory becomes dynamically governable.

## Narrative Payoffs

Nathaniel Cole's refusal and Sarah Navarro's witness can destabilize manufactured history.

## Production Notes

Use seamless updates, altered citations, and disappearing discrepancies rather than obvious censorship.
