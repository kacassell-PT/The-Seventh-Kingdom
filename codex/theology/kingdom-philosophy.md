# Kingdom Philosophy

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** August 2026
**Depends On:** `../../canon/SERIES_BIBLE.md`; `../../showrunner/KINGDOM_DENSITY.md`

The Kingdom cultivates rather than controls. It forms people through communion, truth, worship, memory, craftsmanship, hospitality, slowness, courage, and love.

Its practices include the Long Table, Houses of Memory, inherited song, patient work, care for children and elders, and the treasuries of bread, time, and talent.

## Historical Consequences

The Kingdom appears as a viable counter-civilization rather than merely private belief.

## Narrative Payoffs

Readers encounter what is worth saving before the story reaches restoration.

## Production Notes

Kingdom spaces should show repair, inheritance, texture, shared work, meals, and living growth.
