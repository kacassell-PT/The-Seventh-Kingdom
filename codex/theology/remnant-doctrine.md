# Remnant Doctrine

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** August 2026
**Depends On:** `../../canon/CONSTITUTION.md`; `../../showrunner/MASTER_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md`

The Remnant is not an elite class that earns grace through usefulness. It is a people preserved for faithful witness, formation, hospitality, truth, worship, and rescue.

## Guardrails

- Ordinary people must matter.
- Prophetic function does not determine human worth.
- Resistance must not reproduce Babylon.
- The Fellowship rescues rather than dominates.
- Faithfulness is embodied in costly action.

## Historical Consequences

Community, not exceptional power, becomes the durable form of resistance.

## Narrative Payoffs

The Twelve can include people no strategic institution would choose.

## Production Notes

Avoid superhero-team framing, triumphal poses, and utilitarian treatment of minor characters.
