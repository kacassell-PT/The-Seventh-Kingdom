# Seven-Kingdom Theology

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** August 2026
**Depends On:** `../../canon/CONSTITUTION.md`; `../../canon/THEOLOGY_REFERENCE.md`

The title *The Seventh Kingdom* operates within a biblical theology of human kingdoms judged beneath the reign of Christ. The fictional sequence must not be presented as a newly revealed prophetic chart.

The governing destination is established: Jesus Christ returns as King, false sovereignty is exposed, and the story moves beyond collapse into restoration.

## Historical Consequences

Political and technological powers remain creatures, never ultimate subjects of history.

## Narrative Payoffs

The final victory is received from the true King rather than engineered by the Remnant.

## Production Notes

Any detailed kingdom mapping requires Scripture citations, interpretive humility, and Founder theological review.
