# Testament of Light Commentary

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** August 2026
**Depends On:** `fragments.md`; `../../canon/THEOLOGY_REFERENCE.md`; Open Development Question #4

## Governing boundary

The Testament is a fictional witness inside the story. It must never compete with, amend, or supersede Scripture.

## Commentary method

For each fragment, distinguish:

1. exact recovered text;
2. speaker or discoverer's interpretation;
3. institutional interpretation;
4. reader inference;
5. approved narrative meaning;
6. unresolved mystery.

## Open rules

Origin, authority, perception mechanism, limits, concealment, reveal sequence, and relationship to the Twelve remain unresolved.

## Historical Consequences

Interpretations cannot silently harden into canon simply through repetition.

## Narrative Payoffs

Characters may disagree honestly about meaning while the story preserves a coherent eventual answer.

## Production Notes

Use this file for analysis, never for unmarked invention of fragment text.
