# The Rule of Symbols

**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** August 2026
**Depends On:** `../canon/CONSTITUTION.md`; private Kingdom Archives Creative Constitution Article IX

## Water
Surrender, cleansing, passage, baptism, and realities beyond computation.

## Fire
Revelation, Pentecost, purification, and the light that reveals rather than flatters.

## Trees
Patient growth, cultivation, inheritance, and the Gardener's way.

## Stone
Witness, remembrance, covenant, and testimony that survives violence.

## Bread
Communion, hospitality, provision, and strangers becoming family.

## Light
ORACLE generates artificial illumination. Christ brings revealing light. Readers should feel the difference before it is explained.

## The Table
Communion over control; the Kingdom's central civilizational image.

## Historical Consequences

The package's symbolic vocabulary is preserved as development material while established protected meanings remain governed by the private archive.

## Narrative Payoffs

Repeated images can accumulate meaning across books without becoming arbitrary visual decoration.

## Production Notes

Check every symbol against the governed design language before using it in manuscript, cover, screenplay, artwork, or merchandise.
