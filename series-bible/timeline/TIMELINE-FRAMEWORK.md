**Canon Status:** Exploratory
**Version:** 0.1
**Last Reviewed:** July 2026

## Purpose

This framework tracks chronology, causality, duration, and knowledge movement across seven books and future media. It must not imply that the unresolved final-seven-years sequence is settled.

## Timeline Layers

Maintain separate layers:

1. **World chronology:** dated historical and civilizational events.
2. **Series clock:** Book/season position and any final-seven-years countdown.
3. **Character time:** age, travel, recovery, relationships, and subjective duration.
4. **Publication/adaptation time:** drafting, release, and screen continuity; never overwrite story time.
5. **Knowledge chronology:** when each faction learns, believes, hides, or misremembers a fact.

## Event Record

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Event ID | Stable identifier; never recycle |
| Story date | Exact date, range, or `undated—open` |
| Duration | Start, end, and elapsed time |
| Location | Place and jurisdiction |
| Participants | Present, absent, affected |
| Causality | Trigger, event, immediate consequence, downstream consequence |
| Knowledge | Who knows what before/after; source reliability |
| Systems | Consensus, ARGUS, LifeCredit, ORACLE, Fellowship, or other |
| Evidence | Source document and status |
| Dependencies | Open questions or prerequisite events |
| Status | Canon, Provisional Canon, or Exploratory |

## Rules

- Never invent dates to repair a gap; mark the gap and escalate it.
- A timeline event is not canon merely because it appears here.
- Every major event must be tested politically, humanly, and spiritually.
- Track travel, recovery, pregnancy, seasons, communications delay, institutional response, and resource constraints where relevant.
- Distinguish simultaneous events from revealed-later events.
- Preserve mystery by separating what happened from who can prove it.
- Adaptation changes receive a separate record and an explicit source link.

## Current Spine (Source-Based)

The private Master Project Document describes: Information Era (1985–2025), Age of Acceleration (2025–2032), Great Convergence (2032–2038), Age of Delegation (2038–2047), Age of Integration (2047–2054), Age of Consensus (2054–2062), Age of Harmony (2062–2068), and Age of Reconstruction (2068 onward). These are Provisional Canon source claims, not a fully locked production calendar.

The seven-book outline currently maps Calling through The Seventh Kingdom onto the final seven years, but explicitly flags that mapping as blocked by Open Question #6. Treat all countdown dates as exploratory until Karl rules.

## Continuity Gate

Before approving a chapter, episode, or major reference entry, verify: no impossible travel or recovery; no duplicated event; no unexplained knowledge; no broken age or institutional sequence; no contradiction with higher-status sources; and every new dependency is registered.

## Historical Consequences

The studio gains one chronology without erasing uncertainty or confusing narrative reveal order with event order.

## Narrative Payoffs

Causally earned escalation makes the slow convergence believable and lets delayed revelations carry moral weight.

## Production Notes

Keep the master ledger machine-readable where practical. Use stable IDs, ISO dates when known, explicit unknowns, and a separate adaptation column for screen constraints.
